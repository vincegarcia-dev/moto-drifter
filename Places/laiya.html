Laiya Beaches

Laiya is a popular coastal destination in San Juan, Batangas, renowned for its long stretch of creamy white sand and clear waters.
It is a favorite getaway for beachgoers seeking relaxation, thrilling water sports like jet skiing and banana boating, and a variety of beachfront accommodations ranging from budget-friendly to luxurious.

Located San Juan, Batangas
Opening Hours Varies by resort (Day tours typically 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM)
Best Time to Visit November to May (Dry Season)
