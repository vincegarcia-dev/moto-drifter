Venice Grand Canal Mall

Venice Grand Canal Mall is an Italian-inspired lifestyle and open-air mall located in the heart of McKinley Hill, Taguig City.
It is famous for its architectural replica of the Venetian canals, offering romantic gondola rides, diverse international dining options, and vibrant street performances.

Located McKinley Hill, Taguig City, Metro Manila
Opening Hours 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM
Best Time to Visit Late Afternoon to Evening (for gondola rides and night lights)
Entrance Fee Open to Public (Gondola ride is approx. ₱500)
Type Lifestyle Mall / Tourist Attraction
