Bonifacio Global City

Bonifacio Global City (BGC) is a modern, clean, and pedestrian-friendly financial and lifestyle district in Taguig, Philippines.
It is known for its upscale shopping, vibrant culinary scene, striking public art murals, green open parks, and buzzing nightlife.

Located Taguig City Metro Manila
Opening Hours 11:00 AM - 10:00 PM
Best Time to Visit Morning, Late Afternoon or Evening
Entrance Fee Open to Public
Type Commercial District
